Image capture

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Citrix Components > Citrix Workspace > Remoting client devices

Scope

Machine

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Citrix\ICA Client\Engine\Lockdown Profiles\All Regions\Lockdown\Virtual Channels\Image Capture

Description

Use this policy to enable and restrict the remote application or desktop's access to scanners, webcams, and other imaging devices on the client device.
