Enable WPAD optimization

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

Setting the policy to Enabled or leaving it unset turns on WPAD (Web Proxy Auto-Discovery) optimization in Google Chrome. Setting the policy to Disabled turns off WPAD optimization, causing Google Chrome to wait longer for DNS-based WPAD servers. Whether or not this policy is set, users can't change the WPAD optimization setting.
