Default sensors setting

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome > Content settings

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

Setting the policy to 1 lets websites access and use sensors such as motion and light. Setting the policy to 2 denies access to sensors. Leaving it unset means AllowSensors applies, but users can change this setting.
