ADMX Guide

Include non-standard port in Kerberos SPN

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome > HTTP authentication

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

Setting the policy to Enabled and entering a nonstandard port (in other words, a port other than 80 or 443) includes it in the generated Kerberos SPN. Setting the policy to Disabled or leaving it unset means the generated Kerberos SPN won't include a port.
