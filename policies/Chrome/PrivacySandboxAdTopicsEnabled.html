Choose whether the Privacy Sandbox Ad topics setting can be disabled

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome > Privacy Sandbox policies

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

A policy to control whether the Privacy Sandbox Ad topics setting can be disabled for your users. If you set this policy to Disabled, then the Ad topics setting will be turned off for your users. If you set this policy to Enabled or keep it unset, your users will be able to turn on or off the Privacy Sandbox Ad topics setting on their device. Setting this policy requires setting the PrivacySandboxPromptEnabled policy to Disabled.
