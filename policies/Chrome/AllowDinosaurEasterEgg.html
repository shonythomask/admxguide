Allow Dinosaur Easter Egg Game

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

Setting the policy to True allows users to play the dinosaur game. Setting the policy to False means users can't play the dinosaur easter egg game when device is offline. Leaving the policy unset means users can't play the game on enrolled Google ChromeOS, but can under other circumstances.
