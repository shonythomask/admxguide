Abusive Experience Intervention Enforce

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

If SafeBrowsingEnabled is not Disabled, then setting AbusiveExperienceInterventionEnforce to Enabled or leaving it unset prevents sites with abusive experiences from opening new windows or tabs. Setting SafeBrowsingEnabled to Disabled or AbusiveExperienceInterventionEnforce to Disabled lets sites with abusive experiences open new windows or tabs.
