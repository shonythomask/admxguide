Enable AutoFill for credit cards

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome - Default Settings (users can override)

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome\Recommended

Description

Setting the policy to True or leaving it unset means users can control autofill suggestions for credit cards in the UI. Setting the policy to False means autofill never suggests or fills credit card information, nor will it save additional credit card information that users might submit while browsing the web.
