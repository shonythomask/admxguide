ADMX Guide

Enable Google Cast

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Google Chrome > Google Cast

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Google\Chrome

Description

Setting the policy to Enabled or leaving it unset turns on Google Cast, which users can launch from the app menu, page context menus, media controls on Cast-enabled websites, and (if shown) the Cast toolbar icon. Setting the policy to Disabled turns off Google Cast.
