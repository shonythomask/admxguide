ADMX Guide

Blocked Sites

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Firefox > Cookies

Scope

Both

Registry Key

HKLM\Software\Policies\Mozilla\Firefox

Description

If this policy is enabled, cookies are blocked for the origins indicated. If a top level domain is specified (http://example.org), cookies are blocked from all subdomains as well. If this policy is disabled or not configured, cookies are not blocked by default.
