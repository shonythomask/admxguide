Turn off revision tracking

Category

User Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 > Collaboration Settings > Co-authoring

Scope

User

Registry Key

HKCU\software\policies\microsoft\office\16.0\powerpoint\coauthoring

Description

This policy setting allows you to turn off revision tracking in PowerPoint. By default, revision tracking is on in presentations where revision tracking is supported. If you enable this policy setting, revisions made to a presentation aren’t tracked or shown. If you disable or don’t configure this policy setting, revisions made to a presentation are tracked and shown.
