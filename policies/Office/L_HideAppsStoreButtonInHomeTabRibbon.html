Hide the Office Store button

Category

User Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft Outlook 2016 > Outlook Options > Other

Scope

User

Registry Key

HKCU\software\policies\microsoft\office\16.0\outlook\options\webext

Description

The Office Store button allows users to get apps for Outlook from the Home tab in the ribbon. If you enable this policy setting, the Office Store button doesn’t appear on the Home tab in the ribbon. If you disable or don’t configure this policy setting, the Office Store button appears on the Home tab in the ribbon.
