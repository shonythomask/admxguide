Disable Weather Bar

Category

User Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft Outlook 2016 > Outlook Options > Preferences > Calendar Options

Scope

User

Registry Key

HKCU\software\policies\microsoft\office\16.0\outlook\options\calendar

Description

This policy setting allows you to turn on or turn off the Weather Bar. If you enable this policy setting, the Weather Bar is turned off. If you disable or do not configure this setting, the Weather Bar is turned on.
