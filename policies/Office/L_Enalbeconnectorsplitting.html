Enable connector splitting

Category

User Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft Visio 2016 > Visio Options > Advanced > Editing Options

Scope

User

Registry Key

HKCU\software\policies\microsoft\office\16.0\visio\application

Description

When you place a shape on the line of a connector, it splits and each piece becomes a separate connector glued to the shape. Not all drawing types support connector splitting.
