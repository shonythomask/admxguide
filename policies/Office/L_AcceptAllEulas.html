ADMX Guide

Accept all EULAs

Category

User Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft Office 2016 > Privacy > Trust Center

Scope

User

Registry Key

HKCU\software\policies\microsoft\office\16.0\registration

Description

By default, users are required to accept a EULA upon activating an Office license. By setting this policy, all EULAs will be automatically accepted machine-wide and no prompts will be shown.
