ADMX Guide

Disable local content safety to and from the Summarization with References scenario for the computer.

Category

Computer Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft Office 2016 (Machine) > AI > Content Safety > Specific > Summarization with References

Scope

Machine

Registry Key

HKLM\software\policies\microsoft\office\16.0\common\ai\contentsafety\specific\summarizationwithreferences

Description

This policy setting disables local content safety of the Summarization with References scenario for the computer. For local content safety policy, scenario-specific settings are prioritized over general settings and computer settings are prioritized over user settings. - If this policy setting is enabled, local content safety for the Summarization with References scenario is disabled for the computer. - If this policy setting is disabled, local content safety for the Summarization with References scenario is enabled for the computer. - If this policy setting is not configured, local content safety for the Summarization with References scenario is determined by lower priority policy settings. - If this policy setting is not configured and lower priority policy settings are also not configured, local content safety for the Summarization with References scenario is enabled for the computer. For this policy setting, the order of priority is: 1. Disable local content safety to and from the Summarization with References scenario for the computer. 2. Disable local content safety in general for the computer. 3. Disable local content safety to and from the Summarization with References scenario for the user. 4. Disable local content safety in general for the user.
