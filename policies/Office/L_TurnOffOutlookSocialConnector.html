Turn off Outlook Social Connector

Category

User Configuration > Administrative Templates > Microsoft Outlook 2016 > Outlook Social Connector

Scope

User

Registry Key

HKCU\software\policies\microsoft\office\outlook\socialconnector

Description

This policy setting allows you to turn off the Outlook Social Connector. If you enable this policy setting, the Outlook Social Connector is turned off. If you disable or you do not configure this policy setting, the Outlook Social Connector is turned on.
